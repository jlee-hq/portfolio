About

I’ve spent my career at the intersection of product strategy and live-service games — nine years building the systems that keep a game economy healthy while keeping players happy. Most of that has been at Riot Games, where I’ve led product for VALORANT since its earliest days, and before that helped shape League of Legends’ monetization strategy. Before games, I worked in enterprise software and technology consulting, which is where I picked up a habit for grounding creative decisions in the numbers behind them.

Outside of work I’m usually deep in VALORANT, Dota 2, Counter-Strike 2, or whatever the latest Mass Effect-style RPG is. I follow the NBA and run a fantasy team every year, enjoy strategy board games, and I’m always hunting down a new LA taco truck.
